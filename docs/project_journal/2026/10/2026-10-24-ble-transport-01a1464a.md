# Authenticated Bluetooth LE Transport and Benchmark

## Summary

- Add an explicit Bluetooth LE control route for previously QR-paired iOS
  devices, plus an authenticated no-op round-trip benchmark for Wi-Fi and BLE.

## Current State

- The Mac advertises a per-Mac service UUID as a scan hint. BLE authentication
  uses the existing paired 32-byte token as a PSK; message traffic is encrypted
  and sequence checked. The token is not sent over BLE.
- Transport changes disconnect deliberately. Control messages are not retried
  or silently moved between transports. Older Macs without the probe capability
  remain usable while the benchmark is disabled.
- The benchmark performs three warm-ups and 90 sequential measured exchanges.
  The phone reports its own monotonic-clock RTT statistics; the Mac reports
  local pong-preparation time without cross-clock subtraction.
- `tooling/swift/check.sh` passed strict formatting and lint, 78 AppleCore
  tests including pure advertising lifecycle coverage, a macOS app build, a
  generic iOS Simulator app build, and generated Info.plist checks.
- An iOS Simulator UI regression covers the disabled benchmark state when an
  older Mac omits the probe capability. The fixture does not exercise BLE
  radio, pairing credentials, desktop input, or real audio.
- No physical Bluetooth radio or device acceptance was performed.
- Canceled and timed-out probe identities remain bounded across benchmark
  restarts, so matching late replies do not close a healthy connection. The
  shared result formatter includes minimum, maximum, p50, p95, and jitter.
- Failed BLE handshakes release the retained central slot. Bluetooth power
  transitions invalidate stale session callbacks and re-publish the service
  when the system requires rebuilding its local GATT database.
- Advertising readiness follows Core Bluetooth's asynchronous success callback
  and active-state property. A failed start gets one delayed retry; lifecycle
  resets cancel it, and a second failure leaves advertising stopped.
- Simulator tests retain all functional assertions with a 240-second per-test
  allowance. The draft-preservation case reached its final checks only after
  about 117 seconds of successful actions, leaving insufficient time under the
  former 120-second limit. The UI step and Apple job are bounded to 40 and 50
  minutes respectively.

## Next Steps

- Verify pairing, reconnect, transport switching, control delivery, and
  disconnect on a supported physical iPhone/iPad and Mac. Record hardware
  acceptance separately from package tests, Simulator UI, and app builds.
